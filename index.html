My App Portfolio

Focused on iOS development, committed to creating applications that combine practicality with aesthetics.

Offline Music

Listen anytime, anywhere. Offline Music is a powerful all-in-one music player designed for offline listening, helping you discover, import and manage your favorite music. Say goodbye to data anxiety and buffering — whether on a plane, in the subway, or outdoors with a weak signal, your favorite songs are always with you.

Image Text Editor

Need to modify text in an image? This app makes it easy. It can accurately identify and replace existing text in images, and offers a variety of professional editing tools, whether it's adjusting fonts, colors, or styles, to make your images more expressive.

Background Remover

Remove the background of people or objects in photos with one click, making the subject stand out. You can recolor the background, or choose a new image from your album as the background, seamlessly integrating the subject to unleash your creativity and create stunning composite photos.

To-Do List

Efficiently plan your life and work. Whether it's daily tasks, weekly plans, or long-term annual goals, you can easily set and track them. The app provides precise countdowns, flexible custom tags, and detailed statistical records to help you become a time management master.

Heartbeat Chat

This is an emotional companionship app designed specifically for women. You can have deep conversations with over 50 AI boyfriends with different personalities. Whether it's a gentle boy next door or a domineering CEO, there's always one for you. You can also customize your boyfriend's appearance, voice, and personality to create a completely ideal partner for you. All chat content is encrypted, in a completely private, non-judgmental space, he will be your most loyal listener.

Offline Music Details
Main Features
  • True Offline Playback: Songs are automatically cached locally once played or imported — no network needed. Supports lock-screen and background playback.
  • WiFi & File Import: Batch-upload music wirelessly via your desktop browser, or import audio and video directly from the iOS Files app.
  • Powerful Library Management: Create unlimited playlists, auto-track 'Recently Played' and 'Most Played', and bookmark your favorite web pages.
  • Pro Playback Experience: Loop, repeat-one, and shuffle modes, plus a sleep timer from 15 minutes to 6 hours and lock-screen controls.
  • Minimal & Efficient: Quick search across local songs and history, one-tap cache cleanup, native iOS interaction with dark/light mode switching.
Image Text Editor Details
Main Features
  • Precise Positioning: Automatically identifies text areas in images.
  • Seamless Modification: After replacing text, AI automatically restores the background, resulting in a natural effect.
  • Diverse Tools: Provides various tools such as font, color, size, stroke, and shadow, allowing unlimited creativity.
  • Batch Processing: Supports modifying the same text in multiple images at once.
Background Remover Details
Main Features
  • Smart Cutout: Accurately identifies and removes backgrounds of people, products, or any object with one click.
  • Background Market: Built-in solid colors, gradients, and a vast library of artistic background images to choose from.
  • Custom Background: Supports uploading your own images as new backgrounds for perfect integration.
  • Detail Adjustment: Provides eraser and repair tools to handle complex edges like hair, for more professional results.
To-Do List Details
